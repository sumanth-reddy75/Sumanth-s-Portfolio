Warm-start optimization for the Day-Ahead Market solver

The energy-market solver rebuilt its MILP from scratch every day even though the problem barely changed. I designed and benchmarked two warm-start strategies, reusing yesterday's solution (D-1) and last week's same-weekday solution (D-7), feeding prior solver states in as an MIP start across 96 time blocks.

Test 3

Test 1
70.9%

67.2%
Test 7

Runtime reduction vs. a cold start, four of eight benchmarked cases. D-1 warm start won in 75% of cases; 40–50% speed-up on average.

  • The hard part was the failure mode: warm starts silently broke when a contract changed the problem's structure even though every variable bound still checked out. I traced it to a constraint the old solution never accounted for and built the safeguard: validate first, fall back to cold start on any doubt.
  • MinIO persistence for solver states, Ray for parallel benchmarking, automated tests over the eight cases.

IIT HyderabadRuns fully offline

KathaMitra: an AI storytelling mentor that never leaves the phone

An Android app for 9–12-year-olds that turns screen time into Socratic reflection: narrated stories from Indian epics, then open-ended questions the child answers out loud. Inference, retrieval, speech-to-text and text-to-speech all run on-device, which is what makes it DPDP Act 2023 compliant by construction.

  • Quantized Gemma 2B on MediaPipe / LiteRT with a local sqlite-vec index; end-to-end response under 450 ms, a 60% reduction from the first build.
  • A "Symmetric Context" retrieval step pulls clean English narrative concepts from code-switched Hindi-English speech before querying the index, then fuses them back with the child's own words so answers stay in their voice and grounded in verified stories.
  • Scored 4.80 / 5.0 in pedagogical evaluation; design-thinking research and focus groups with children informed the interaction model.
